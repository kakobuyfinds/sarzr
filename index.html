How to use an ACBuy spreadsheet

Use the catalog to find a candidate, then check the original seller listing and your agent’s order page for the exact variant and the total cost.

Confirm the listing

Open the catalog entry and follow its seller link if available. Match the color, size, and variant to the original listing. Compare the seller’s measurements with a similar item you own.

Review QC photos

If warehouse photos are available, check the ordered color and size label. For shoes, compare both sides and the outsole. For clothing, check the seams and ask for a measurement photo if the fit is uncertain.

Compare the total cost

Add the item price, domestic delivery, agent fees, international shipping, and any applicable import charges. Check whether your chosen shipping line bills by actual or volumetric weight.

ACBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is an ACBuy spreadsheet?

It’s a shopping directory used to find products, compare listings, and collect seller links for review before placing an agent order. This guide features a selection from HipoBuyFind.

Can I use a HipoBuyFind link in ACBuy?

Open the HipoBuyFind entry and look for the original marketplace link. If there is one, paste that original link into ACBuy to see whether the listing is supported and available.

Does the USD estimate include shipping?

The estimate covers the listed item price. Get the shipping quote for your destination and parcel size before deciding whether the total fits your budget.

What if a listing is unavailable?

Search the full HipoBuyFind catalog by product name or category. Compare seller details, sizing and variants before replacing an item on your shortlist.
